📊 Copilot Credit history beyond 30 days is here.

The Power Platform admin center keeps 30 days of Copilot Credit consumption. The new Copilot Credit usage reports API (public preview) lets you keep it as long as you want.

What caught my attention:
🔹 FeatureDetailsPerResource: credits by agent, environment, product and feature
🔹 BillingDetailsPerEnvironment: prepaid vs. pay-as-you-go per billing policy
🔹 3 ways in: Power Platform for Admins V2 connector, pac copilot-studio, or REST
🔹 The tutorial builds a weekly workflow that emails the rows above 1,000 credits

Agent FinOps, running in Copilot Studio itself.

⚠️ Public preview: delegated user auth only (no service principal), admin role required, no sovereign clouds.
